## Scene Realism Guidelines & Visual Safeguards

1. **Scene 1 (Hook & Intro):** Maintain eye contact with smartphone lens. Keep shaker bottle resting naturally in one hand while introducing the tub.
2. **Scene 2 (Scoop & Rapid Mix):** Macro camera focus on fine powder dissolving into water; show authentic liquid swirl and crema layer forming.
3. **Scene 3 (Taste Reaction):** Ensure realistic throat swallow movement before the smile and eye widening reaction.
