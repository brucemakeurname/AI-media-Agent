# UGC AI Realism Review — PVL ISO Gold Post-Workout Shake Hack

## Realism Diagnosis
- **Current Risk:** AI gym renders risk looking overly glossy, synthetic skin texture, perfectly clean non-gym backgrounds, and distorted shaker / product interactions.
- **Fix:** Ground prompts in raw handheld iPhone POV, visible post-workout sweat/flushed skin, authentic Singapore commercial gym ambient clutter (benches, rubber flooring, gym bag), and single clean actions per clip.

## 7T Check (UGC Realism Standard)
- **Thật người (Real Person):** ✅ 28–32yo Singaporean gym-goer, natural athletic build, consistent dark tank top & sweatband across all sequences.
- **Thật việc (Real Activity):** ✅ Authentic post-workout routine: bench rest, water pour, scoop add, rapid shaker dissolve, taste reaction, packing into gym duffel.
- **Thật tay (Real Hand Interaction):** ✅ Natural grips on shaker bottle, lid twist, and pointing to label macros without complex multi-finger gymnastics.
- **Thật mặt (Real Facial Expressions):** ✅ Natural post-workout fatigue -> casual speaking -> satisfied smile after sipping -> friendly concluding smile.
- **Thật giọng (Real Voice & Tone):** ✅ Energetic Singlish gym bro cadence (`lah`, `ah`, `tahan`), exact approved dialogue locked from Gemini TTS.
- **Thật cảnh (Real Environment):** ✅ Modern Singapore fitness gym with subtle background depth-of-field, realistic ambient lighting and lived-in equipment.
- **Thật máy (Real Camera Work):** ✅ Handheld selfie / chest-level iPhone framing with subtle organic micro-jitter and natural breathing movements.

## Negative Prompt Reference
Avoid distorted hands, extra fingers, warped product label, stiff body, frozen face, unnatural eye contact, sliding feet, overacting, perfect studio lighting, cinematic commercial look, glossy ad style.
