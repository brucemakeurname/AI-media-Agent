# Creative Brief — Ultimate Sup Top Picks Carousel

- Core message: Find your next sports-nutrition staple at Ultimate Sup.
- Desired response: Swipe the carousel, recognize three available products, then visit Shopee Singapore.
- Audience: Singapore sports-nutrition shoppers.
- On-image copy: English only. No prices, offers, dates, certifications, nutrition claims, or efficacy claims.
- Product facts: product names only, sourced from packshot filenames. No SKU/flavour/serving/price supplied.
- CTA: `Shop Ultimate Sup on Shopee.sg`
- Visual system: black/yellow high contrast; one locked template for slides 2–4.
- Open design questions: exact approved logo use remains `REVIEW REQUIRED`, so use text wordmark only.
