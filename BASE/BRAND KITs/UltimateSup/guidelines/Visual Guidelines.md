# Ultimate Sup Visual Guidelines

**Status:** WORKING DRAFT — REVIEW REQUIRED

## Ecommerce and Social Direction

- Lead with one customer tension, one verified product angle, and one CTA.
- Keep the retailer logo visually separate from product-brand packshots and manufacturer logos.
- Use the existing Ultimate Sup homepage templates only as layout mechanisms. Their historical
  offer text, dates, vouchers, products, gifts, and claims are never current facts.
- Add exact campaign copy, SGD prices, and offer conditions in post-production, then verify against
  the ticket.

## Layout

- Preserve safe margins and keep legal/offer conditions legible at final platform size.
- Prefer a high-contrast black/yellow heading system with white or pale-yellow information panels.
- Use one dominant packshot or product group; avoid a crowded catalog unless the template explicitly
  supports it.
- Use `assets/elements/*_draft-v1.svg` only as optional visual cues after approval. They are not
  official logo components.

## Photography and Thumbnails

- Use sharp, authentic product packshots from `../Product/`; do not alter labels or variant names.
- TikTok thumbnail captures in `../assets/thumbnail/` are composition and channel-style references
  only. Treat all visible text as historical.
- Avoid shame, fear, exaggerated body transformations, medicalised digestion/acne messaging, and
  misleading before/after implications.

## Quality Check

Before export, verify logo treatment, product/variant, legibility, spelling, SGD amounts, offer
eligibility, safe-area crop, CTA, and claim safety against the active ticket.
