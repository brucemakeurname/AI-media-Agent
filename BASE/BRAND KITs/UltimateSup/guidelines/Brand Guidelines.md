# Ultimate Sup Brand Guidelines

**Status:** WORKING DRAFT — REVIEW REQUIRED

## Brand Role

Ultimate Sup is a Singapore sports-nutrition retailer. It curates and sells multiple product
brands; it is not the manufacturer of Mutant, PVL, NutraBio, or any other stocked brand.

## Identity Assets

- Use the candidate horizontal mark in `../assets/logo/` only after brand-owner approval.
- The store mark is visually black and yellow with a shield/bodybuilder icon and lightning motif.
- Do not redraw, stretch, recolour, crop, or place the logo over low-contrast imagery.
- The TikTok profile image in `../assets/avatar/` is a channel reference, not automatic permission
  to use as a campaign avatar.

## Working Visual Tokens

- **Core contrast:** black, bright yellow, pale yellow, and white.
- **Use:** black/yellow for hierarchy and promotional emphasis; white or pale yellow for readable
  offer panels; product-brand colours only as secondary product cues.
- **Typography direction:** bold, condensed display treatment for short headings; simple sans-serif
  for conditions, units, and CTA. Confirm any exact typeface with the brand owner.

## Retailer and Claim Guardrails

- State product facts, serving sizes, prices, offers, and availability only from the active ticket
  and approved Singapore source.
- Do not elevate a product-brand certification, result, testimonial, or ingredient fact into an
  Ultimate Sup brand claim.
- Never reuse a price, voucher, date, free gift, or CTA shown in `assets/thumbnail/` as a current
  campaign fact.

## Approval Needed

Confirm the final logo package, exact colour values, approved fonts, logo clear-space rules, and
allowed social-avatar usage before marking this document `APPROVED`.
