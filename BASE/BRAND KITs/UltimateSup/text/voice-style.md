# Ultimate Sup Voice Style

**Status:** WORKING DRAFT — REVIEW REQUIRED

- Use clear, energetic Singapore-market English. Use Singlish only when the active ticket approves
  it and the wording remains easy to understand.
- Start from a real training, recovery, convenience, taste, value, or stock-selection tension; do
  not manufacture urgency, scarcity, reviews, or transformations.
- Keep product facts precise. State only the approved product/variant, source-supported value, and
  one clear CTA.
- Use short headings and direct CTA language. Keep conditions, prices, and dates exact rather than
  conversational or implied.
- Do not use disease-treatment claims, guarantees, “safe for everyone”, “no side effects”, “HSA
  approved”, or unverified certification/comparison claims.

## Channel Notes

- **TikTok:** conversational hook, one product angle, basket/shop CTA when approved.
- **Shopee Singapore:** precise product/offer wording, SGD currency, conditions visible.
- **Instagram:** benefit-led but factual; keep claim support and product identity explicit.
