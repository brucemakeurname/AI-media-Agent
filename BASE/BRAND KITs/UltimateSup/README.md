# Ultimate Sup Brand Kit

## Purpose

This is the permanent retailer-level source library for Ultimate Sup. It is not a campaign output
folder: save campaign briefs, creative files, QA, and final assets in
`BASE/CAMPAIGNs/<campaign-slug>/`.

## Inventory

- `Product/`: existing multi-brand product packshots. Preserve the current filenames.
- `voice/`: existing local voice references and transcript.
- `../1. Creative_Prompt_Template/Brand_Template/UltimateSup/Homepage sup 8.8.26/`: existing
  11 image/JSON template pairs. This is a shared layout-reference library, not campaign output.
- `assets/`: official or publicly observed identity assets. Check `assets/asset-manifest.json`
  before using an asset.
- `guidelines/`: working brand and visual guidance. These documents are operational drafts unless
  a brand owner marks a section approved.
- `text/`: safe default channel tone and draft hashtag groups.

## Approval Rules

- `APPROVED`: confirmed by the Ultimate Sup brand owner for the stated use.
- `REVIEW REQUIRED`: collected from an official public channel or created as a draft; do not use
  in publication until approved.
- `HISTORICAL REFERENCE ONLY`: may inform visual direction but cannot supply current price, offer,
  product fact, date, CTA, or claim.

Ultimate Sup is the retailer. Do not present a product manufacturer, a product logo, or a product
claim as Ultimate Sup's own brand identity.
