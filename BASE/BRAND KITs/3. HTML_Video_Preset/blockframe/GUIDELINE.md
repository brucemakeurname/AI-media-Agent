# BlockFrame video bundle

Warm parchment `#E9E5DB`, deep indigo `#1B2566`, and solar yellow `#F1EE2E` form a restrained art-catalogue system. Every scene carries a sun bloom and bottom-right pagenum; text and hairlines remain indigo.

- Display: Instrument Serif 400; body/micro: Archivo; data/pagenum: JetBrains Mono.
- No shadows, rounded cards, or borders heavier than 1px.
- Keep 55–60% of a scene open. Use a yellow panel only as a decisive structural block.
- Optional image bands are real article imagery only; they are muted and bounded by hairlines.
- All type sizes use `var(--v-text-scale)` and all CSS is scoped to this preset.
