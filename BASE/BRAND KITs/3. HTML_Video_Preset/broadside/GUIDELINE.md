# Broadside video bundle

Broadside is a flat protest-poster system: only ink-black `#111111` and fire-orange `#E85D26` are
frame registers. Massive lowercase Barlow is the primary graphic object; IBM Plex Mono is reserved
for uppercase chrome, numbering, and labels.

- Use one register per frame: cream text on dark, or ink text on orange. Cream is never a third ground.
- No gradient, radius, card elevation, or shadow. Structure is carried by 1px hairlines.
- Keep one display statement dominant and preserve 45–55% silence on declarative frames.
- Data and comparison frames may be denser, but retain orange as the only accent and cap bullet lists at three.
- Image-capable scene types retain optional `imageUrl` slots only; all type sizes are scaled by `var(--v-text-scale)`.
