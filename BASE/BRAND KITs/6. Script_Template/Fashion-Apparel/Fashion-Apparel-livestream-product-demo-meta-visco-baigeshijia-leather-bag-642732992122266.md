## Arc Breakdown
`message-stack`, livestream/presenter register. No problem beat and no character arc — it opens
straight on the product and stacks reassurance claims (price-vs-perception → leather quality →
warranty → capacity → versatility → matching) proven by a hands-on tabletop demo, then a full-body
worn look. Two-block structure: a **hands-only tabletop crush-and-fit demo** (~0–14s) establishing
durability + capacity, then a **presenter-to-camera hard-sell** (~14–72s) that re-shows the same bag
at every angle, on-body, while the VO runs the attribute stack and closes to inbox. VO is a
first-person "colleague-envy" framing rather than a scripted narrative.

## CTA Analysis
No landing-page link and no spoken URL — the entire conversion push is a **Messenger inbox close**
(`cta_type: MESSAGE_PAGE`, button "Send message"). Urgency lives in the ad copy (a 50%-off + free-ship
"today" framing) rather than in an on-screen end-card; the video itself carries no burned-in offer
graphic, so the discount is a caption-layer claim the DM conversation is meant to convert on. This is
the Vietnamese social-commerce "chốt đơn qua inbox" pattern: sell the product fully in-video, then
move the transaction into a 1:1 chat, not to a website.

## Conversion Mechanics
Sells against one objection — "cheap bag = low quality" — by stacking rebuttals: a price-perception gap
opener (coworkers thought it cost millions), a physical crush-and-recover durability proof shown twice
(tabletop, then on-camera), an explicit leather warranty claim, then capacity + versatility +
easy-matching claims, closed on light social proof. The single durability claim is *shown* (twist the
bag, it springs back) rather than only asserted — the strongest beat in the ad. No performance numbers
are exposed by Meta, but a ~14-month continuous run is a soft longevity signal. Library value is the
**structure**: a two-block livestream-demo template (hands-only crush/fit proof → presenter worn-demo
attribute stack → inbox close) that any accessory/product-showcase ticket can field-substitute into.
