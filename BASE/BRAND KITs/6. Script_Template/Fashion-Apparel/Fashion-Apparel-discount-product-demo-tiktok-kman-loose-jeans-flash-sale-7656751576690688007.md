## CTA Analysis
No spoken CTA and no persistent badge during the demo — the entire conversion push is concentrated in
the final ~6s end-card: "FLASH SALE" + per-variant "SALE" price tags + a style code (to order the
exact item). Urgency = flash-sale framing (caption states a 55%-off/"today only" discount). The
Conversions objective + landing page (`kman.fun/jean-a3-kman`) confirm this is a direct-response ad;
the offer is withheld until after the fit is sold, a demo-then-offer order.

## Conversion Mechanics
Sells one benefit — a flattering straight-loose fit that "fits every body" (caption: sizes 45–95kg) —
proven by wearing it across many angles (visual repetition of the single fit claim) with a
reassurance-stacking VO, THEN converts on a flash-sale discount with clear colorway variants + a style
code for frictionless ordering. Core claim (straight-loose universal fit) is hammered via ~4 different
worn angles rather than restated in words. Mid-tier metrics (CTR "Top 46% of industry", low raw likes)
fit a workmanlike conversion ad, not a viral one — its library value is the *structure*, not reach.
