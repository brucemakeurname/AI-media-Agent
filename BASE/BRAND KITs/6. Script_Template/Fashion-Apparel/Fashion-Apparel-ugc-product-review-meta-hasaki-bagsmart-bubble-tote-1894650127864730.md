## Conversion Mechanics
A feature-review that de-risks a considered purchase (a laptop tote) by *showing* each claim: outer
pockets tilted to camera, interior opened to reveal the 15.6" laptop sleeve, padded handle squeezed for
the soft/light/water-resistant claim, variant inset for choice, unisex framing to widen the buyer. The
laptop-sleeve reveal is the load-bearing beat — it's the single feature that turns a generic tote into a
work/travel bag. UGC presenter register + clean studio + lifestyle B-roll build "real person reviewed
this" trust, then hand off to the retailer product page. No performance numbers exposed by Meta; broad
placement (FB/IG/Audience Network/Threads) signals a reach-oriented buy. Library value is the
**structure**: a retailer-UGC feature-review template (presenter intro → interior/feature reveal with
B-roll → variants + store CTA) reusable for any considered-purchase accessory.
