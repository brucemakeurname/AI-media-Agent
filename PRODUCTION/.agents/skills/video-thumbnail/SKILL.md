# video-thumbnail

The thumbnail is a production deliverable, not a generic prompt-search exercise. Derive one
clear frame from the locked script/ticket and render it with the approved image tool.

## Required brief

Write `node/thumbnail-brief.md` with:

- one short approved hook/title (exact spelling and diacritics);
- the actual presenter/character/product from the video;
- one concrete action or visual tension from the first 1–2 seconds;
- 9:16 composition, safe text area, and the brand/product reference paths;
- no invented price, offer, review, claim, logo, or before/after implication.

Render `thumbnail.jpg` at the campaign root with `acad-image-gen` (or the approved equivalent),
then verify the product/variant, hook, legibility, safe area, and first-frame prepend. Do not call
`creative-direction` to invent a disconnected thumbnail query.
