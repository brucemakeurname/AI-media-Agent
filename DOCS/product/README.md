# Product Documentation — Scope & Publication Status

Last updated: 2026-08-11

> Product files in this directory are internal working references. They help research, training, and creative planning; they do not independently authorize public claims.

Product documentation is part of the workspace source layer, but it does not replace the active campaign ticket, current Singapore label/listing, or written product-owner approval.

## Required Check Before External Use

For each product statement, comparison, testimonial, price, flavour, promotion, certification, ingredient, nutrition value, or efficacy angle, confirm the current approved source in this order:

1. The active `Ticket.md` and written approval.
2. Current on-pack label and approved Singapore listing.
3. Approved product owner confirmation.

If that confirmation is missing, remove the statement or label it `REVIEW REQUIRED`. Never turn a training example into a medical, safety, regulator-approval, or guaranteed-outcome claim.

## Content Draft Guardrails

- Treat legacy hooks and personas as creative inputs, not copy to publish verbatim.
- Rework any acne, digestive-condition, intolerance, body-result, competitor, rating, or testimonial language into approved claim-safe copy before use.
- Preserve source date and original file when extracting a fact for a research deliverable.

## Workspace Placement

- Product references stay in `DOCS/product/`.
- Campaign-specific fact extracts, review notes, and approval evidence stay in the active production unit's `node/`.
- Final campaign assets stay in `BASE/CAMPAIGNs/[IP] Campaign/[Platform]/[Format]/[Date Folder]/`; do not save them beside product references.
