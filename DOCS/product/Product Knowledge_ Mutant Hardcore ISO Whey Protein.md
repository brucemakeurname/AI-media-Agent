### 2\.1\. The Core Technology: Hydrolyzed \+ Isolate

Unlike standard whey proteins, Mutant ISO Surge uses a dual\-phase formula\.

- **Phase 1: Whey Protein Isolate \(WPI\):**

    - **Process:** Filtered using **Microfiltration**\. Microfiltration is often employed in conjunction with ultrafiltration and nanofiltration to achieve bacteria removal and fat separation\. The process effectively separates protein from unwanted fats and carbohydrates\.

    - **Result:** This removes almost all fat, sugar, and lactose while keeping the protein structure "bioactive" \(alive\) to support the immune system\. It provides a product with a high protein content and low lactose\.

- **Phase 2: Hydrolyzed Whey Protein \(WPH\):**

    - **Process:** The protein chains are pre\-digested \(broken down\) into tiny peptides using enzymes\.

    - Imagine a piece of beef is a **solid brick wall**\. Your stomach takes 4 hours to break it down\. Hydrolyzed Whey is like **loose sand**,** **it requires zero effort to digest and floods the bloodstream immediately\.

    - **Benefit:** "Express Delivery" to muscle fibers\. Instant recovery\. No heavy stomach feeling\.



### 2\.2\. The Asian Stomach Solution \(Digestive Enzymes\)

This is the **\#1 USP** for the Asian market, where most adults are Lactose Intolerant\.

- **The Component:** Added **Lactase** and **Protease** enzymes\.

- **Mechanism:**

    - **Lactase:** Acts like a pair of scissors, cutting up any remaining lactose sugar so it doesn't ferment in the gut \(preventing gas/bloating\)\.

    - **Protease:** Helps break down the protein even further for maximum absorption\.

- **The Selling Point:** Born for Asian Digestion\. Even if you cannot drink milk, you can drink it\. It eliminates the fear of diarrhea caused by indigestion\.



### 2\.3\. Nutritional Analysis \(The "Real Protein" Standard\)

Hardcore ISO does not use "Amino Spiking" \(cheap fillers like Glycine/Taurine to fake protein content\)\.

- **Protein Content:** **25g** per serving \(nearly **80%** per serving \- a very high ratio\)\.

High prices don't always guarantee high absorption\. The true power of protein lies in its bioavailability, which is proven by a complete EAA and BCAA profile\.

- **Full 9 EAAs \(Essential Amino Acids\) \+ Glutamine:** **16\.3g** per serving\.

    - *Analysis:* **EAAs \& Glutamine** make up **65%** of the total protein weight\. **EAAs** make up **46%** of the total protein weight\. This is the standard ratio found in natural beef\. If a brand seems to spike their whey, this number drops to under 30%\.

    - *Benefit:* True muscle synthesis\. You are paying for actual building blocks, not filler\.

- **BCAA:** **5\.5g** \(Leucine, Isoleucine, Valine\)\. Activates the mTOR pathway to switch the body from "Muscle Breakdown" to "Muscle Building"\. Decides 80% the efficiency of EAAs\.



If protein does not seem to be effective, maybe it is used against muscle mechanics\.

**Muscle Mechanics: How It Works**

Training creates "micro\-tears" \(cracks\) in muscle fibers\. Post\-workout, the body screams for materials to fix these cracks\. Protein rushes into the bloodstream \(Hydrolyzed speed\) and fills the cracks with high\-quality bricks \(EAA/BCAA\)\.

**The Result:** Muscle heals **thicker** and **stronger**\.

**The Rule:** No training = No cracks = No growth\.
